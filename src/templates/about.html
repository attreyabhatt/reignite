About TryAgainText

TryAgainText is a web tool for thinking through dating conversations. Paste an exchange or upload a screenshot to get reply ideas, then choose and edit the wording yourself. FlirtFix is our companion Android app.

The guides are published by TryAgainText. They offer practical examples for common texting moments; they are not professional counselling, evidence of a particular person's intentions, or a promise that someone will reply.

How our examples are prepared

We use AI assistance to draft and organise examples, then check the published text for relevance, clarity, repeated wording, unsupported claims and respect for boundaries. The byline identifies the publisher; it does not claim that a named relationship expert wrote or endorsed the advice.

Conversations in guides and demonstrations are fictional illustrations. They are not customer messages, testimonials, research findings or predictions of how another person will respond. Demonstrations label illustrative reply output on screen.

We add or update guides when they answer a distinct question, improve an explanation, or reflect a useful correction. Dates on reviewed guides refer to substantive editorial changes or first publication, rather than changing automatically every day.

Use the ideas in your own voice

Only use details that are true for you. Match the familiarity of the relationship, accept a refusal, and respect requests for space. No wording can manufacture another person's interest.

Remove private details you do not want to share before using the tool. Read our privacy policy for information about processing and retention.

Corrections and questions

If an example is unclear or a guide needs a correction, contact TryAgainText with the page URL and the issue. We welcome specific feedback.
